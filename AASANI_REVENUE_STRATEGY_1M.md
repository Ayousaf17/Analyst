## PRICING PHILOSOPHY

### Why These Prices?

**$397 Toolkit:**
- Low enough to be "no-brainer" for SMBs
- High enough to filter serious buyers from tire-kickers
- Generates $71K Year 1 (covers marketing costs)

**$5,500 Assessment:**
- 2-3x higher than strategic plan ($2,500-5,000)
- Reflects 20 hours of expert work
- Qualifies serious buyers (if they won't pay $5.5K for diagnosis, they won't pay $25K for solution)
- Single price (not range) = no negotiation confusion

**$25,000/$45,000/$75,000 Phase 2:**
- Clear tiers eliminate "what will it cost?" confusion
- Small ($25K) = single department/workflow (8 weeks)
- Medium ($45K) = 2-3 departments (12 weeks)
- Large ($75K) = full company (16 weeks)
- Based on value delivered, not hours (you get faster = you make more)

**$3,500/month Retainer:**
- Includes 10 hours/month of optimization work
- Priced at $350/hour effective rate (premium positioning)
- 8 clients × $3,500 = $28K MRR = stability

### What if deals slip or don't close?

**Built-in buffers:**
1. Revenue target = $1,166,000 (16.6% over $1M goal)

   - Toolkit → Assessment: 20% (industry avg is 25-30%)

- Increase toolkit sales (easier to scale than projects)
- Run promotion: "Assessment + Phase 2 bundle" = $28,000 (save $2,500)
- Add one more large deal ($75K) = instant catch-up
