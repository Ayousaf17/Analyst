# SECTION 10: FAQ

## Section Header

```
Headline: Questions We Get

```

## FAQ Items

### Q1: "Why can't we skip straight to AI implementation?"
```
Short answer: Because AI on bad foundations fails. Every time.

Long answer: We've seen it dozens of times. A business invests
$50,000+ in an AI solution, only to abandon it within 6 months
because the underlying data was messy, the workflows didn't
support it, or the team never adopted it.

Phase 1 assessment isn't us being difficult—it's us protecting
your investment. If your foundation is solid, the assessment
confirms it and we move fast. If it's not, we save you from an
expensive mistake.
```

---

### Q2: "What if we've already done our own assessment?"
```
Great! Share it with us. If it covers what we need to know, we'll
adjust our scope accordingly. We're not trying to duplicate work—
we're trying to make sure we have accurate information before
designing your systems.

That said, most internal assessments focus on symptoms, not root
causes. We often find issues that weren't on anyone's radar.
```

---

### Q3: "How long until we see results?"
```
Phase 1 (Assessment): 2-3 weeks to complete, immediate clarity
Phase 2 (Build): 6-12 weeks, results visible during implementation
Phase 3 (Smart Systems): Varies by complexity

You won't wait 6 months to see if things are working. We implement
in sprints with visible progress at each stage. Most clients see
meaningful improvements within the first month of Phase 2.
```

---

### Q4: "What makes you different from other AI consultants?"
```
We don't lead with AI. Most consultants want to sell you the
shiny thing. We want to make sure the shiny thing will actually
work for you.

Our background isn't in technology sales—it's in operations,
process design, and organizational change. We understand that
successful implementation is 20% technology and 80% everything else.
```

---

### Q5: "What if we're not ready for any of this?"
```
Then we'll tell you that. The quiz exists for a reason—to help
both of us figure out if now is the right time.

If you're in the middle of a merger, your leadership is in
transition, or you don't have budget allocated, we're not going
to push you into an engagement. We'd rather wait until you're
set up for success than take your money when we can't deliver results.
```

| Element | Specification |

| **Typography** | Clean sans-serif, good hierarchy, readable |
| **Layout** | One-page scroll, clear sections, generous whitespace |
| **Mobile** | Fully responsive, touch-friendly CTAs |

## Doodle Style Guidelines

**DO:**
- Black line art on cream backgrounds
- Stick figures for people
- Simple, minimal detail
- Playful-yet-professional tone
- Hand-drawn aesthetic

**DON'T:**
- Overly detailed illustrations
- Multiple colors or gradients
- Robotic/sci-fi AI imagery
- Stock photo aesthetic
- Complex scenes
