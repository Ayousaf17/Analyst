## PART 2: THE NUMBERS TO USE (Only These)

**Research validated dozens of statistics. Use only these 3-4 on your website:**

| Stat | Where to Use It | Why It Works |
|------|-----------------|--------------|
| **"85% of AI projects fail"** | Hero section, Problem section | The big, memorable number. Everyone's heard AI hype—this cuts through. |
| **"Only because of broken foundations, not bad technology"** | Pair with 85% stat | Shifts blame from AI to foundations. Validates your positioning. |
| **"2.7x more likely to succeed with human-in-the-loop"** | Solution section (optional) | If you mention HITL, this backs it up. |
| **Save client-specific numbers** | Case studies only | "15 hours/week saved" > generic percentages. Real results from real clients. |

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't list 10 statistics on the homepage
- Don't cite sources in body copy (save for blog posts)
- Don't use stats that require explanation

## PART 3: TONE & VOICE (The "Aasani" Sound)

### Sound Like:
- A trusted advisor, not a salesperson
- Plain-spoken, not corporate
- Confident but humble
- Calm in chaos
- Educational, not condescending

### Don't Sound Like:
- Tech bro ("disruptive," "game-changing")
- Academic ("operationalize," "leverage")
- Hype machine ("Transform your business!")
- Finger-pointing ("Your processes are broken")

### Language Rules:
- Short sentences (12-15 words average)
- One idea per sentence
- Use "we" and "together"
- Metaphors from everyday life (foundations, duct tape, tangled wires)
- If a 12-year-old can't understand it, simplify

## PART 4: THE QUIZ (Lead Magnet)

### Structure: 12 Questions Total
- 4 questions: People Readiness
- 4 questions: Tools & Workflows
- 4 questions: Data Quality

### Sample Questions (Plain English):

**People:**
1. How many employees have received AI training this year?
2. When AI comes up in meetings, what's the typical reaction?
3. If AI saved 10 hours/week, could your team use that time effectively?
4. Who makes technology decisions in your business?

**Tools:**
1. How often do employees manually copy data between systems?
2. What's your biggest technology frustration right now?
3. Do your tools "talk to each other" or work in silos?
4. Do you currently pay for any AI tools?

**Data:**
1. How confident are you that your business data is accurate?
2. How easy is it to find last quarter's sales data right now?
3. Where do you keep your most important business information?
4. Who has access to your business data?

## PART 6: WHAT NOT TO DO

### On the Website:
- Don't list more than 3 statistics
- Don't use jargon that requires explanation
- Don't hide pricing behind "Contact Us"
- Don't make every section a sales pitch
- Don't overwhelm with options (one clear CTA per section)

### In Your Messaging:
- Don't lead with fear ("Your competitors are winning!")
- Don't promise magic ("10x your revenue!")
- Don't talk down to prospects
- Don't sound like every other consultant

### In Your Services:
- Don't skip Phase 1 for eager clients
- Don't build AI on broken foundations (even if they pay)
- Don't offer "unlimited" anything
- Don't compete on price
