# Aasani Systems: Practical Marketing Plan for First-Time Founder
**Budget: $500/month or less | Target: SMBs tired of AI hype**
**Created:** December 13, 2025

---

## Reality Check First

You're a first-time business owner in Austin, TX with:
- Zero case studies (initially)
- Limited marketing budget ($500/month)
- No marketing experience
- A Framer website at aasani.ai
- Workflow Detangler as your lead magnet

**The good news:** Your positioning ("Structure Before Intelligence") cuts through AI hype noise. Your target market is drowning in broken systems and desperate for honest help.

**The challenge:** You need leads NOW, not in 6 months. This plan focuses on speed and practicality.

## 1. FOCUS ON 2-3 CHANNELS (Don't Spread Thin)

### PRIMARY CHANNEL: LinkedIn (Organic)

**Why LinkedIn First:**
- Your buyers (SMB founders, COOs, operations managers) are there daily
- Free to start, algorithm favors engagement over follower count
- B2B trust signal - professional content performs better than social platforms
- You can reach decision-makers directly

**Time Investment:** 10 hours/week
**Budget:** $0 (organic only for first 90 days)

**What to Post (5x per week):**

**Monday:** Carousel (8 slides)
- Example: "7 Signs Your Team is Drowning in Tool Chaos"
- Create in Canva (free tier)
- Include quiz CTA in comments

**Tuesday:** Short video (60-90 seconds)
- Record on phone, use CapCut (free) to add subtitles
- Topics: "Why we don't start with AI", "The $40K chatbot mistake", "What 'AI-ready' actually means"
- Casual, founder-to-founder tone

**Wednesday:** Text post with story
- Share a real interaction (anonymized): "A prospect asked me yesterday..."
- End with question to drive comments
- No CTA - just engagement

**Thursday:** Workflow Detangler result share
- "This week, 23 people took our quiz. Here's what we learned..."
- Share aggregate insights (keeps it valuable, not salesy)
- CTA: "Take the quiz yourself"

**Friday:** Case study or "anti-hype" post
- Initially: "Here's why we DON'T recommend AI to most businesses"
- Later (with clients): "How [Client] saved 15 hours/week without AI"

**Weekend:** Reshare your best-performing post from the week with new commentary

**LinkedIn Engagement (30 min/day):**
- Comment meaningfully on 10 posts from your target audience
- Join 3 groups: "Small Business Operations", "AI for Business", "Austin Tech Community"
- DM value-first to people who engage with your content (NOT cold pitches)

---

### SECONDARY CHANNEL: Email (Owned Audience)

**Why Email Second:**
- You own the list (algorithm can't kill your reach)
- Highest ROI for B2B ($42 return per $1 spent)
- Critical for nurturing Workflow Detangler leads

**Time Investment:** 3 hours/week
**Budget:** $0-25/month (Mailchimp free up to 500 subscribers, then $13/mo)

**Email Strategy:**

**Weekly Newsletter (Every Friday):**
- "Systems Thinking" - 200-word insight + 1 useful link + quiz CTA
- Subject line examples: "You can't automate chaos", "Why your tools don't talk to each other"
- Plain text only (no fancy design - feels personal)

**Automated Nurture Sequences (see Section 4 for details):**
- Quiz result → 5-email sequence over 14 days
- Consultation no-show → 2-email recovery sequence

**Growth Strategy:**
- Add signup box on blog posts ("Get weekly systems insights")
- LinkedIn bio: "Subscribe to my newsletter for AI reality checks"
- Quiz completion → auto-add to list

**Goal:** 100 subscribers in Month 1, 300 by Month 3

---

### TERTIARY CHANNEL: SEO/Blog (Long Game)

**Why Blog Third:**
- Takes 6-12 months to compound, but it's evergreen
- Your target searches "AI readiness assessment", "workflow automation SMB"
- Each post becomes a LinkedIn content source

**Time Investment:** 4 hours/week (1 post every 2 weeks)
**Budget:** $0

**Blog Strategy:**

**First 6 Posts (Foundation Content):**
1. "85% of AI Projects Fail - Here's Why" (keyword: AI failure rate)
2. "How to Know If Your Business Is AI-Ready" (keyword: AI readiness assessment)
3. "People → Tools → Data: Why Order Matters" (keyword: business systems design)
4. "What We Mean By 'Structure Before Intelligence'" (branded keyword)
5. "The Hidden Cost of Tool Sprawl" (keyword: SaaS tool management)
6. "Why Your Team Resists Automation (And How to Fix It)" (keyword: change management AI)

**SEO Basics (Free Tools):**
- Use Ubersuggest (free tier) to find keywords
- Write 1,200-1,500 words per post
- Include internal links between posts
- Add meta description and title tag
- Compress images before uploading (fast load times)

**Distribution:**
- LinkedIn post promoting blog (Tuesday or Friday)
- Email newsletter feature (every other week)
- Repurpose into LinkedIn carousel (1 blog = 3 carousels)

---

### CHANNELS TO AVOID (for now)

**DON'T START:**
- Twitter/X - Wrong audience, too time-intensive
- Facebook/Instagram - B2C-heavy, low business intent
- TikTok - Conflicts with "anti-hype" positioning
- YouTube - Great later, but video editing takes 8+ hours per video
- Paid ads - Wait until you have 50+ quiz completions to learn what resonates

**Reason:** Dilution kills momentum. Master 2-3 channels before expanding.

## 3. HOW TO USE WORKFLOW DETANGLER TO CAPTURE LEADS

### The Detangler Setup (Based on Your Spec)

**What it is:** AI-powered workflow analysis tool that returns structured JSON output
- User describes a messy workflow
- Tool analyzes friction across People, Tools, Data
- Returns readiness score + 3-step fix

**Your Advantage:** No competitor has this. It's educational AND qualifies leads.

---

### Lead Capture Strategy

#### Step 1: Embed on Homepage (Primary CTA)

**Hero Section:**
- Headline: "Is Your Workflow Secretly Costing You $10K/Month?"
- Subheadline: "Describe any workflow. Get instant analysis. Free, no email required."
- CTA Button: "Analyze My Workflow" → Opens Detangler

**Why no email gate initially?**
- Lower friction = 3x more people will use it
- You capture leads AFTER they see value (on results page)

#### Step 2: The Results Page (Lead Capture Moment)

**What users see after analysis:**

```

[Display their analysis from JSON output]

Diagnosis: "Triple-entry across email, spreadsheet, and invoicing means errors slip through unnoticed."

Detected Friction Points:
- Data: Order details manually copied from email to spreadsheet
- Data: Same information re-entered into invoicing software
- People: Handoff between two people creates gap where orders get missed
- Tools: Email, spreadsheet, invoicing system aren't connected

Your 3-Step Fix:
1. Map the order journey [Details]
2. Create one entry point [Details]
3. Add a verification checkpoint [Details]

---

[NOW THE LEAD CAPTURE]

Want a Custom Roadmap for YOUR Business?

Our Foundation Assessment ($5,500) includes:
✓ Deep-dive workflow mapping across your entire operation
✓ Tool audit and integration architecture
✓ Data health score and cleanup plan
✓ 60-day implementation roadmap

[PRIMARY CTA: "Book Free 15-Min Consultation"]
[SECONDARY CTA: "Email Me This Analysis" → Captures email, adds to nurture sequence]
```

**Why this works:**
- They've already gotten value (free analysis)
- Results page shows you understand their pain
- Two CTAs for different intent levels (hot = book call, warm = get email)

## 5. SHOULD YOU RUN PAID ADS? (Budget Considerations)

### Short Answer: NOT YET

**Why wait on paid ads:**

1. **Budget is too tight** - $500/month barely covers tools, not ads
2. **No proven offer yet** - You need 50-100 organic leads first to know what resonates
3. **Organic has room to grow** - You haven't maxed out LinkedIn/email yet
4. **Creative costs** - Good ads need 3-5 variations to test = design time/money

**When to start paid ads:**
- Month 4-6, after you have 3-5 clients and proven messaging
- Budget increases to $1,000+/month
- You've maxed out organic reach (500+ LinkedIn connections, posting 5x/week for 90 days)

---

### If You MUST Run Ads (Against Advice)

**Best option with $500/month: LinkedIn Retargeting**

**Strategy:**
- Target: People who visited your website but didn't complete Detangler
- Ad creative: "You started analyzing your workflow - finish in 30 seconds"
- Budget: $15/day ($450/month)
- Expected: 50-100 clicks, 5-10 Detangler completions

**How to set up:**
1. Install LinkedIn Insight Tag on website
2. Create retargeting audience (website visitors, last 30 days)
3. Run "sponsored content" ad with CTA to Detangler
4. A/B test 2 ad variations

**Expected CPA (Cost Per Lead):** $30-60
**Confidence:** MEDIUM (5/10) - Small budget limits testing

---

### Better Use of $500/Month (Alternative to Ads)

**Option 1: Tools That Scale Organic**
- Canva Pro ($13/mo) - Better design templates
- Descript ($12/mo) - Easy video editing with AI
- Typeform ($25/mo) - Professional quiz/forms
- Mailchimp Standard ($13/mo) - Email automation
- **Total: $63/mo** | **Remaining: $437 for reserve**

**Option 2: Outsource Content Creation**
- Fiverr: LinkedIn carousel designer ($25 per carousel)
- Upwork: Video editor ($50 per video)
- Budget: 4 carousels + 2 videos = $200/month
- **ROI:** 10 hours saved = you can do sales calls instead

**Option 3: Pay for Warm Intros**
- Buy lunch/coffee for referral partners ($50 each)
- Target: Austin tech community, Vistage groups, EO chapter
- Goal: 10 lunches/month = 10 warm intro opportunities
- **ROI:** One client = $5,500-$75K (way better than ads)

**Recommendation: Option 3** - Relationship-based selling beats ads for high-ticket B2B

## 6. HOW TO BUILD CREDIBILITY WITH ZERO CASE STUDIES

### The Cold Start Problem

**You need proof, but you have no clients yet. Here's how to build credibility anyway:**

---

### Tactic 1: Use Your Own Workflow as Case Study

**"How I Built Aasani's Operations (Meta Case Study)"**

**Content angle:**
- "Before starting Aasani, I audited my own business systems"
- Document YOUR workflow for: client onboarding, project management, invoicing
- Show the before (chaos) → after (clean)
- Be vulnerable: "Here's what I got wrong"

**Where to publish:**
- LinkedIn post series (5 parts, one per week)
- Blog post: "The Founder's Workflow: How I Practice What I Preach"
- Newsletter feature

**Why it works:** Transparency builds trust. You're not claiming expertise you don't have.

---

### Tactic 2: Offer "Beta Pricing" for First 5 Clients

**Positioning:**
- "I'm building Aasani's portfolio. The first 5 clients get 50% off in exchange for:"
  - Detailed case study rights
  - Video testimonial
  - Logo use on website
  - Willingness to be reference for future clients

**Pricing:**
- Phase 1 normally $5,500 → Beta price $2,750
- Phase 2 normally $25,000 → Beta price $12,500

**Where to promote:**
- LinkedIn post: "Looking for 5 businesses to partner with"
- Austin tech Slack channels
- Warm network email

**Why it works:** Lower price = lower risk for them. You get proof + revenue.

---

### Tactic 3: Publish "Workflow Audits" (Anonymized)

**Strategy:**
- Offer free 30-minute workflow audits (no strings attached)
- Do 20 of these in Month 1-2
- Write them up as anonymized case studies:
  - "Company A: E-commerce with 15 employees"

**Publish as:**
- Blog series: "Workflow Audit #1-20"

- Newsletter content

**Why it works:**
- You demonstrate expertise without claiming results
- Readers see themselves in the problems
- Creates FOMO: "They got a free audit, I want one too"

### Tactic 4: Leverage "Borrowed Credibility"

**Partner logos:**

**Founder credentials:**
- Your background (be specific): "10 years in [industry], built systems for [types of companies]"

**Why it works:** Association with known brands transfers trust

### Tactic 5: "Live Build" Content Strategy

**Concept:**

- LinkedIn posts get <50 impressions → Engagement is too low, comment more on others' content

- Fewer than 200 Detangler uses → Major pivot needed (maybe direct outreach vs. inbound)
