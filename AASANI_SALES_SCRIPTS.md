## Script 5: Discovery Call Opening (First 2 Minutes)

**Start with permission + context:**

```
"Hey [Name], thanks for taking the time. I've got us down for 30 minutes—does that still work?

Cool. So here's how I usually run these: I'm going to ask you about what's actually broken in your workflows. I'll probably take some notes. And then I'll be honest about whether I think we can help or not.

Sound good?

Quick context on me: I run Aasani Systems. We help SMBs fix their workflow foundations before they waste money on AI. Most companies try to automate chaos—we help you build structure first.

So let's start here: What made you reach out? What's the thing that's driving you crazy right now?"
```

**Then listen. Let them talk for 5-10 minutes.**

**Follow-up questions to have ready:**
- "Walk me through what that looks like on a typical day."
- "Who else is involved in that process?"
- "What have you already tried to fix this?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would this look like?"
- "What happens if you don't fix this in the next 6 months?"

**How to transition after listening:**

```
"Okay, I'm hearing [summarize their pain points]. Here's what I think:

[If it's a fit:]
This is exactly what we help with. The typical path is: we'd do a 2-week assessment to map out where the friction actually is. You'd get a clear roadmap of what to fix first, second, third. Then you decide if you want us to build it or if you want to DIY it. Make sense to walk through what that looks like?

[If it's not a fit:]
Honestly, I don't think we're the right fit for this. What you're describing is [too small / too specific / needs a different kind of specialist]. Here's who I'd recommend instead: [give them a referral]. Does that make sense?"
```

## Script 6: Handling "What Do You Charge?" Early in Conversation

**Context:** They ask about pricing before you've learned anything about their situation

**Option A (Deflect to value):**

```
"Good question. It depends on scope, but let me give you context:

Our Foundation Assessment is $5,500. Takes 2 weeks. You get a full map of what's broken and a roadmap to fix it.

Then if you want us to build it, that starts at $25K and goes up depending on how much we're fixing.

But here's the thing—I don't know yet if we're even the right fit for you. Can I ask you a few questions first so I'm not wasting your time?"
```

**Option B (Transparent with ranges):**

```
"I'll be straight with you:

Phase 1 (assessment): $5,500
Phase 2 (building the fix): $25K-$75K depending on scope
Phase 3 (AI, if you want it): Custom, only after Phase 2

Most clients start with Phase 1 to see what's actually broken. Then they decide.

Does that ballpark work, or is this outside your budget? I'd rather know now so we're not wasting each other's time."
```

**Option C (ROI frame):**

```
"Fair question. Here's how to think about it:

Our typical client discovers they're losing $40K-$100K per year to workflow friction. Hidden costs—manual work, errors, delays, duplicate effort.

Our assessment costs $5,500. Implementation starts at $25K.

So if we find $75K in annual waste and charge you $30K to fix it, that's paid back in 6 months. Then it compounds every year after.

Make sense? Want to talk through what you think you're losing right now?"
```

```
"That's actually the smartest thing I've heard all week.

Here's what most companies do: they see the AI hype, they buy some tool, they try to implement it, and it fails because their data is a mess and their workflows are chaotic.

What we do is the OPPOSITE. We help you get AI-ready by fixing your foundations first. Then, when you ARE ready, the AI actually works.

So you're not behind—you're ahead of 95% of companies who are doing this backwards."
```

**Option C (Use it as qualifier):**

```
"Perfect. Neither are we.

We don't sell AI implementations. We sell the boring foundation work that comes BEFORE AI.

Here's the question: Is your team spending hours every week on manual, repetitive work? Copy-pasting data? Chasing down information? Fixing errors from disconnected systems?

If yes, we can help with that—no AI required.

If no, then honestly, you probably don't need us yet."
```

## CONFIDENCE BOOSTERS FOR FIRST-TIME FOUNDERS

### When you feel like you're bothering people:

**Remember:** You're not selling them something they don't need. You're helping them see a problem they already have but might not know how to articulate.

### When they say "let me think about it":

**Say this:** "Totally fair. What would you need to see or know to make this a clear yes or no? I'd rather help you decide than leave this hanging."

### When you feel like an imposter:

**Remember:** You know more than they do about workflow optimization. They're drowning in manual work and don't know how to fix it. You do. That's not arrogance—that's your job.

### When someone asks a question you don't know:

**Say this:** "Good question. I don't know off the top of my head, but let me look into that and get back to you by [specific time]. What's the best way to reach you?"

### When pricing feels awkward:

**Remember:** Your $5,500 assessment will likely uncover $40K+ in annual waste. You're not expensive—you're an investment that pays for itself.

## WHAT NOT TO SAY (Red Flags)

Don't say:
- "I can transform your business" ❌
- "AI will revolutionize everything" ❌
- "You're doing this all wrong" ❌
- "Everyone needs this" ❌
- "This will only take a few weeks" (if it won't) ❌

Do say:
- "I can help you see where the friction is" ✅
- "AI works when you have clean foundations" ✅
- "This is really common—and fixable" ✅
- "This might be a fit if you're dealing with [specific pain]" ✅
- "This typically takes 8-12 weeks" (if it does) ✅
