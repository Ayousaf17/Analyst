## Executive Summary

Austin's SMB ecosystem is PERFECT for Aasani's "Structure Before Intelligence" positioning. The city has a high concentration of fast-growing companies (10-100 employees) experiencing chaos from rapid growth, with decision-makers accessible through a tight-knit business community. The AI hype is strong here (tech capital of Texas), creating both opportunity and skepticism.

**Key Insight:** Austin SMBs are drowning in tools they bought during COVID and hypergrowth. They need systems cleanup BEFORE adding more AI.

## 1. PRIORITY INDUSTRIES IN AUSTIN (With Pain & Budget)

### Tier 1: HIGHEST PRIORITY (Go After These First)

#### Industry #1: Healthcare Tech / Health Services SMBs
**Why Austin:** Major hub for health tech startups, medical device companies, and digital health platforms. Dell Medical School ecosystem spawned dozens of SMBs.

**Pain Points:**
- HIPAA compliance requires clean data architecture (your strength)
- Multiple patient management systems that don't talk to each other
- Manual data entry between EHR, billing, scheduling, CRM
- AI hype pressure ("we need AI chatbots for patient intake")
- Employee burnout from repetitive admin work

**Budget Reality:**
- These companies have MONEY (VC-backed or revenue-positive)
- Compliance pressure = budget availability
- Average Phase 1 budget: $5-10K (not price sensitive)
- Average Phase 2 budget: $25-50K

**Company Examples in Austin:**
- Consensus Cloud Solutions (healthcare tech)
- Well (digital health platform)
- WellQ (employee wellness tech)
- Hundreds of smaller clinics, digital health startups, med device distributors

**Decision Timeline:** 3-6 weeks (compliance urgency accelerates)

**Confidence: HIGH (9/10)** - This is your #1 target vertical

**Why Austin:** Rapid population growth = boom in professional services firms (lawyers, accountants, consultants).

- High billable hour pressure = need efficiency

**Budget Reality:**

- Consulting/architecture: $5-8K Phase 1, $15-35K Phase 2

**Company Examples:**

**Decision Timeline:** 2-4 months (slower, consensus-driven)

**Confidence: HIGH (8/10)** - Conservative buyers, but once convinced, they buy

## 3. COMPANY SIZE SWEET SPOT

### Ideal Target: **25-75 Employees**

**Why This Range:**

**Too Small (<10 employees):**
- Still using founder's brain as "system"
- No budget
- Can survive on duct tape solutions
- Will try to DIY

**Too Big (>100 employees):**
- Already have internal IT/Ops teams
- Want enterprise consultancies (Deloitte, Accenture)
- Longer sales cycles (6-12 months)
- Politics, procurement processes

**Sweet Spot (25-75 employees):**
- ✅ **Pain is acute:** Chaos is visible, impacting growth
- ✅ **Budget exists:** $25-75K projects are approved regularly
- ✅ **Decision speed:** 1-3 decision-makers, not committees
- ✅ **Accessible:** Founders/COOs still reachable
- ✅ **Retention:** Sticky clients (will buy Phase 2, Phase 3, retainers)

**Secondary Target: 10-24 employees (if fast-growing)**
- Only pursue if they've raised funding or are profitable
- Look for companies that doubled headcount in last 12 months

**In Austin:**
- ~3,000 companies in 25-75 employee range
- ~5,000 companies in 10-24 range (filter for fast-growth)

**Confidence: HIGH (9/10)**
